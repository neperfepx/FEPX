.. _citing:

User Guidelines
---------------

If you use FEPX for your own work, please mention it explicitly in your reports (papers, books,
talks, etc.) and cite the following papers:

- *R. Quey and M. Kasemer, The Neper/FEPX project: free / open-source polycrystal generation,
  deformation simulation, and post-processing, IOP Conference Series: Materials Science and
  Engineering 1249, 012021 (2022)*;

- *P.R. Dawson and D.E. Boyce, FEPX -- Finite Element Polycrystals: Theory, finite element
  formulation, numerical implementation and illustrative examples, arXiv:1504.03296
  [cond-mat.mtrl-sci] (2015)*.
