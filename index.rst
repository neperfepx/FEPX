FEPX: Finite Element Polycrystal Plasticity
+++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++++

.. grid:: 1 1 2 2
   :gutter: 3

   .. grid-item::
      :columns: 12 12 8 8

      .. image:: imgs/home-fepx.png
         :class: fepx-hero

   .. grid-item::
      :columns: 12 12 4 4
      :child-align: center

      FEPX is a free / open-source finite element software package for polycrystal plasticity.  It models the global and local mechanical behaviors of large polycrystalline aggregates with complex microstructures, from elasticity to large plastic strains, in a scalable parallel framework.  It works hand in hand with Neper, which generates the polycrystals and their meshes and post-processes the results.

.. note::

    See also `Neper <https://neper.info>`_, FEPX's companion program: a software package for polycrystal generation, meshing and post-processing, which generates FEPX's meshes and post-processes its results.

FEPX is currently developed by the Advanced Computational Materials Engineering Laboratory `ACME Lab <https://acmelab.ua.edu>`_ at The University of Alabama, USA, and CNRS, France.
